Our Story

Rekdi opened in February 2026 in Poblacion, Makati. Built around grilling, drinking, and long conversations. The food starts with Filipino pantry cooking — rice, noodles, bread, skewers, seafood, things eaten with your hands, things best shared over drinks. Some dishes stay close to memory. Others wander through travel, cravings, street food, and bad decisions. We cook with smoke, butter, chicken oil, lard, stock, acid, sweetness, char, and anything that tastes like something. Nothing here tries to be perfect. Only worth coming back for.
