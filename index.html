OUR
STORY

Rekdi opened in February 2026.

The food started from cravings, experiments, old pantry habits, travel memories, drinking sessions, and accidental discoveries.

Some dishes came from carinderias. Some from bars. Some from kitchens trying to survive with whatever was left.

Nothing here tries to be delicate. Or proper.

If it works, it stays.
